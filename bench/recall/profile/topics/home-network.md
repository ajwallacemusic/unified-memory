- [stated] Router runs OpenWrt; guests get their own Wi-Fi network
- [stated] NAS is a four-bay Synology with 8 TB drives; its hostname is barnacle
- [stated] Backups run nightly at 2 am with restic to Backblaze B2
- [stated] Home Assistant runs on a Raspberry Pi 5; it drives the thermostat schedule and the basement leak sensors
- [stated] Family photos live in Immich on the NAS
- [stated] Password manager is 1Password on a family plan
- [stated] Remote access is through Tailscale; nothing is exposed to the internet directly
